# Night of the Mutant Cat

A point-and-click adventure made in the spirit of *Day of the Tentacle* — except the thing that drank from the fish tank is a cat, and it is getting bigger.

Three flatmates, four rooms, one freezer. You play all three and they are not equally useful:

- **Wes** hacks things
- **Tank** lifts things
- **Dr. Vic** calms animals

Nobody can do anyone else's job. That is the whole game.

## Play

**Browser, no download:** https://standardp.github.io/night-of-the-mutant-cat/
Also available in Spanish — pick the language on the title screen.

## Controls

Click a verb, then click the thing. Click an item in the inventory, then click a target. The 💡 button gives you the next step if you get stuck.

## Two builds

| Folder | What it is |
|---|---|
| `/` (root) | Vanilla single-file HTML/JS build, 320x200 pixel art, EN/ES |
| `/godot` | Godot 4.7 project — same game, native engine, exported to Web |

The Godot build runs with `godot --path godot/` once the engine is installed. Export templates are needed for a web build.

## Notes

- The art is original, drawn on a 320x200 grid with a 46-colour palette. Same era and resolution as the games it parodies, not a copy of their assets.
- Made as a joke. The cat is fine.
